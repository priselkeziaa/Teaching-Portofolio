02 · Teaching philosophy

How I teach

My teaching philosophy begins with wonder. When children are curious, they ask questions, explore and want to express what they have discovered. I plan warm, structured learning where play, drama, movement and care for others and the world around us all have a place.

Learning through wonder

Curiosity is where learning starts. I invite children to notice, wonder and ask questions, then build lessons around what fascinates them.

Sustainability and caring for our environment

I help children take care of the environment through everyday sustainable practices, so that respect for nature becomes a natural habit.

Emotional intelligence and good character

Understanding feelings, showing kindness and making good choices are as important as academic skills. I use positive guidance, stories and role-play to build empathy and good character.

Drama and self-expression

Through drama, role-play, music and movement, children express themselves, use language with confidence and bring stories to life.

Sensory play and physical development

Sensory play and hands-on activities support hand-eye coordination, fine and gross motor skills and early communication.

Fun games and joyful learning

Games make practice memorable. Children build vocabulary, numeracy and social skills while they play, take turns and celebrate together.

Wondergrove

The values behind my own learning project

Wondergrove is my independent learning project. These are the values that guide it, and they shape the way I teach.

Wonder

Curiosity leads the way, and questions are welcome.

Growth

Every child grows at their own pace and is celebrated along the way.

Expression

Children find their voice through drama, music and movement.

Care

Kindness, good character and respect for people and our environment.

Joy

Playful, hands-on learning that children want to come back to.
